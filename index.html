Managed content services for brands

ShineSplash runs the day-to-day work of getting a brand's content in front of more people: on news and content platforms, and on its social pages.

Content syndication

We prepare, review and deliver brands' articles to content platforms and news apps, under the brand's own name and accounts.

Social page management

We plan, create and post content for brands' Facebook, Instagram and Threads pages, and report on how it performs.

How we work

Every piece of content is checked by a person before it goes out, and it follows each platform's rules. Our clients keep ownership of their accounts and their content.
