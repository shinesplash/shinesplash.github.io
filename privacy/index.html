Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information ShineSplash ("we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers this website, the ShineSplash Console (the web app our staff, contractors and clients sign in to), and the work we do for our clients on content and social media platforms. We are based in Canada.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts. The company that hosts it (GitHub) keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service.

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, e-mail address and message you type. They are stored in the ShineSplash Console (hosted by Google Cloud in Toronto, Canada) and e-mailed to our company inbox so we can reply. To stop the form being misused we keep a scrambled code made from your IP address for a short check on how many messages are sent, never the address itself. We keep contact messages for up to 12 months after our last reply, unless you become a client.

Signing in to the ShineSplash Console

The Console is by invitation only. When you sign in with Google (or, later, Microsoft), we receive only:

  • your name and email address, and whether the email address is verified;
  • a permanent account ID from the sign-in provider, so we know it's you next time.

We do not get your password, and we do not get access to your email, files, contacts or anything else in your account.

While you use the Console we also keep:

  • a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed to use the Console; we use no other cookies;
  • a security log of sign-ins and changes made in the Console (who, what, when, and the IP address it came from);
  • the work you do in it: for example, the reviews you complete and the time spent on them, if you are a reviewer.

Our clients' information

For the brands we work for, we hold the business contact details they give us, their brand information, the content we create or deliver for them, and the reports their platforms provide about how that content performs (for example views, engagement and earnings statements).

Social media accounts we manage (Facebook, Instagram, Threads)

When a client connects its pages to us, with the client's permission we use Meta's tools to publish posts, read page and post statistics (including earnings), and read and reply to comments on the client's pages. Comments can include the public name and words of the person who wrote them. We use this only to run and report on that client's pages. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or combine it across clients to profile people.

How we use information

  • to let the right people sign in, and to keep the Console secure;
  • to deliver our services to our clients, and to report to them and bill them;
  • to meet legal and platform requirements.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept and for how long

Console data is kept on systems we control, with access limited to people who need it. We keep account information while you have access and for up to 12 months after it ends. The security log is kept for at least 12 months. We keep client records for as long as our contract and tax rules require. Information from Meta about a client's pages is deleted within 30 days after the client disconnects us or asks for deletion, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact form or e-mail admin@shinesplash.com and we will reply within 30 days. To delete data that came from Facebook or Instagram, see Delete my data. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell the people who use the Console.
