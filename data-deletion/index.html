Delete my data

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time.

How to ask

  1. Send us a message with the contact form starting with "Data deletion request" (or e-mail admin@shinesplash.com with that subject).
  2. Tell us the email address or the Facebook or Instagram name the request is about, and, if you know it, the page or account involved.
  3. We confirm we received it within 3 business days, and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will tell you when it's done.

Facebook, Instagram and Threads

If you are a business that connected its pages to ShineSplash, you can also remove our access yourself at any time: in Meta Business Suite go to Settings → Business apps (or in Facebook, Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations) and remove ShineSplash. We then delete the data we received from Meta about those pages within 30 days.

If you commented on a page we manage, your comment lives on Facebook, Instagram or Threads; you can delete it there. We remove any copy we hold when you ask us.

What we may keep

We may keep a record that a request was made and completed, and information the law requires us to keep (for example invoices and tax records), for the time the law requires.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
